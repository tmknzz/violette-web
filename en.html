02 / A SMALL ROUTINE

Write. Polish.
Put it back.

Use the Composer to compare your original text
with the result, before putting it back.

  1. 1

    Add your text

    Type or paste the text you want to polish into the INBox.

  2. 2

    Choose an action

    Click an action or use its shortcut. The result appears in the OUTBox.

  3. 3

    Review and insert

    Check the result, then use Insert to put it back in the original text field. Violette does not send it for you.

Inserting text requires Accessibility permission. Compatibility may vary depending on the destination app.
